    <h2>1. Who we are</h2>

    <p>Ahotosoɔ Services Ltd (“Ahotosoɔ”, “we”, “us” or “our”) provides property verification, project oversight, property management and agreed local representation services in Ghana.</p>

    <p>For the personal information described in this notice, Ahotosoɔ will normally act as the organisation responsible for deciding why and how that information is used.</p>

    <p><strong>Privacy contact:</strong> hello@ahotoso.com</p>

    <p>Before commercial launch, our registered office details and any applicable Data Protection Commission registration details will be added here where required.</p>

    <h2>2. What this notice covers</h2>

    <p>This notice applies when you:</p>

    <ul>
      <li>visit or use our website;</li>
      <li>contact us by email, telephone, WhatsApp or another approved company channel;</li>
      <li>request a quotation or service;</li>
      <li>complete an Ahotosoɔ enquiry, intake or authority form;</li>
      <li>appoint us to inspect, monitor, manage or report on a property, project, asset or other agreed matter;</li>
      <li>appear in information that a client, contractor, tenant, professional partner or other person provides to us in connection with a legitimate engagement; or</li>
      <li>otherwise interact with Ahotosoɔ in a business or professional capacity.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>3. Information we may collect</h2>

    <p>Depending on the service and your relationship with us, we may collect:</p>

    <h3>Contact and identity information</h3>
    <p>Your name, telephone number, email address, country of residence and preferred communication method.</p>

    <h3>Enquiry and service information</h3>
    <p>The service requested, property or project location, instructions, deadlines, concerns and correspondence.</p>

    <h3>Property and project information</h3>
    <p>Property details, project records, contractor or supplier information, quotations, invoices, plans, photographs, videos, progress information and other documents relevant to the agreed work.</p>

    <h3>Authority information</h3>
    <p>Information reasonably needed to establish that you are entitled to request access, inspection, management, monitoring or representation. Where stronger evidence is required, we will tell you what is needed.</p>

    <p><strong>Please do not send passports, Ghana Cards, bank records or other sensitive identification documents through a general website enquiry form unless we specifically request them through an appropriate channel.</strong></p>

    <h3>Property-management information</h3>
    <p>Where relevant, tenant contact information, rent and arrears information, maintenance records, access arrangements and approved spending limits.</p>

    <h3>Transaction information</h3>
    <p>Ahotosoɔ invoices, amounts due and paid, payment references and records required for accounting or dispute resolution. Our normal operating model is to avoid unnecessary custody of substantial client project or acquisition funds.</p>

    <h3>Inspection and evidence records</h3>
    <p>Dated photographs, videos, observations, notes, document references and related evidence created or obtained while performing an authorised service.</p>

    <h3>Technical website information</h3>
    <p>Basic information supplied automatically by your browser or hosting infrastructure, such as IP address, browser/device information, request logs and security information. If we later introduce analytics, advertising or non-essential cookies, this notice and any relevant cookie controls will be updated before those tools are used.</p>

    <h2>4. How we obtain information</h2>

    <p>We may receive personal information:</p>

    <ul>
      <li>directly from you;</li>
      <li>from a person you have authorised;</li>
      <li>from contractors, suppliers, tenants, property managers or professional advisers involved in an engagement;</li>
      <li>from public or official records where lawful and relevant;</li>
      <li>from Ahotosoɔ representatives or appointed professionals carrying out authorised work; and</li>
      <li>automatically through website hosting, security and communications systems.</li>
    </ul>

    <p>Where another person gives us information about you, we will use it only where there is an appropriate reason connected with the relevant service or another lawful purpose.</p>

    <h2>5. Why we use personal information</h2>

    <p>We may use personal information to:</p>

    <ul>
      <li>respond to enquiries;</li>
      <li>assess whether we can accept an engagement;</li>
      <li>verify authority and arrange lawful access;</li>
      <li>prepare scopes, quotations and agreements;</li>
      <li>deliver inspections, verification, project oversight, property management or other agreed services;</li>
      <li>coordinate with contractors, suppliers and appropriately qualified professionals;</li>
      <li>create evidence records and client reports;</li>
      <li>communicate findings and obtain client decisions or approvals;</li>
      <li>administer invoices, payments and accounting records;</li>
      <li>prevent fraud, conflicts of interest, bribery and misuse of our services;</li>
      <li>handle complaints, disputes and legal claims;</li>
      <li>maintain the security of our website, systems and records;</li>
      <li>meet applicable legal, regulatory, tax and professional obligations; and</li>
      <li>improve our internal procedures and service quality.</li>
    </ul>

    <p>We seek to collect and use only information that is relevant and reasonably necessary for the purpose concerned.</p>

    <h2>6. When information may be shared</h2>

    <p><strong>We do not sell client personal information.</strong></p>

    <p>Where necessary for an authorised engagement or another legitimate business or legal purpose, information may be shared with:</p>

    <ul>
      <li>Ahotosoɔ staff and authorised representatives;</li>
      <li>lawyers, surveyors, engineers, quantity surveyors, valuers and other appropriately qualified professionals;</li>
      <li>approved contractors, suppliers or property-service providers where the client has authorised the relevant work;</li>
      <li>website, email, cloud-storage, communications, accounting or other service providers that support our operations;</li>
      <li>banks and payment providers where required for a transaction;</li>
      <li>insurers, auditors or professional advisers; and</li>
      <li>courts, regulators, law-enforcement bodies or other public authorities where disclosure is required or permitted by law.</li>
    </ul>

    <p>We aim to disclose only the information reasonably required for the relevant purpose.</p>

    <h2>7. Photographs, videos and site evidence</h2>

    <p>Property and project verification often requires visual evidence.</p>

    <p>Where Ahotosoɔ is authorised to perform an inspection or monitoring service, we may create photographs, videos and related records of the relevant site, works, materials, defects, assets or documents.</p>

    <p>Our representatives should avoid unnecessarily capturing people, neighbouring property, private documents or unrelated personal information. Evidence is used for the authorised engagement, quality review, record keeping and, where necessary, complaints or dispute resolution.</p>

    <p>We will not use identifiable client property photographs or project records for public marketing without an appropriate basis or permission.</p>

    <h2>8. Communications with people outside Ghana</h2>

    <p>Many Ahotosoɔ clients may live outside Ghana. Providing our services can therefore involve communications, document exchange or authorised access to information across national borders.</p>

    <p>We will seek to use reputable communications and storage providers and apply reasonable access and security controls. Where applicable law imposes additional requirements on an international transfer or disclosure, we will take appropriate steps before making it.</p>

    <h2>9. How long we keep information</h2>

    <p>We do not intend to keep personal information indefinitely.</p>

    <p>Retention periods depend on the type of record and why it is required. We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:</p>

    <ul>
      <li>provide and document the service;</li>
      <li>maintain financial and accounting records;</li>
      <li>comply with legal or regulatory obligations;</li>
      <li>establish what instructions, approvals or reports were given;</li>
      <li>resolve complaints or disputes; and</li>
      <li>protect the legitimate interests of Ahotosoɔ and its clients.</li>
    </ul>

    <p>When information is no longer reasonably required, it should be securely deleted, destroyed or anonymised in accordance with our records-retention procedures.</p>

    <h2>10. How we protect information</h2>

    <p>We use organisational and technical measures appropriate to the nature of the information we handle. These include controlled access to job records, structured job folders, internal review procedures, conflict controls and measures designed to avoid unnecessary storage of client information on personal devices or informal channels.</p>

    <p>No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If we identify a material security incident involving personal information, we will assess it and take the steps required by applicable law.</p>

    <h2>11. Your choices and rights</h2>

    <p>Subject to applicable law and the circumstances of the request, you may ask us to:</p>

    <ul>
      <li>tell you whether we hold personal information about you;</li>
      <li>provide access to relevant personal information;</li>
      <li>correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;</li>
      <li>explain how particular information is being used;</li>
      <li>consider an objection or concern about the way we are using your information; or</li>
      <li>delete information where there is no longer an appropriate reason to retain it.</li>
    </ul>

    <p>We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request and may retain information where continued retention is required or permitted by law.</p>

    <h2>12. Marketing</h2>

    <p>We will not treat a service enquiry as permission to send unrelated marketing indefinitely.</p>

    <p>If we introduce email or messaging-based marketing, we will provide an appropriate way to opt out of future promotional communications.</p>

    <p>Operational messages about an enquiry, active service, report, payment or client decision are not marketing communications.</p>

    <h2>13. Cookies and analytics</h2>

    <p>At present, Ahotosoɔ should use only website functionality reasonably necessary to operate and secure the site unless additional tools have been deliberately configured.</p>

    <p>If we introduce analytics, advertising technologies or other non-essential cookies, we will review the privacy implications and update our website notices and consent controls where required.</p>

    <h2>14. Complaints and questions</h2>

    <p>If you have a privacy question, want to exercise a right, or believe we have handled your information improperly, please contact us first:</p>

    <p>
      <strong>Ahotosoɔ Services Ltd</strong><br>
      <strong>Email:</strong> hello@ahotoso.com
    </p>

    <p>You may also have the right to raise a concern with Ghana's <strong>Data Protection Commission</strong>, the regulator established under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).</p>

    <p>Official information is available from the Data Protection Commission at:<br>
      <a href="https://dataprotection.org.gh/" rel="noopener noreferrer">dataprotection.org.gh</a>
    </p>

    <h2>15. Changes to this notice</h2>

    <p>We may update this Privacy Notice as Ahotosoɔ's services, systems, legal obligations or data-handling practices change.</p>

    <p>The current version will be published on this page with its latest revision date.</p>
