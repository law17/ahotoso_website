<section class="page-hero"><div class="shell"><span class="eyebrow">How it works</span><h1>We verify. We document. You stay in control.</h1><p>Every Ahotosoɔ engagement follows a clear operating workflow from initial request to final report.</p></div></section>
<section class="section"><div class="shell content-narrow prose"><h2>1. Tell us what you need</h2><p>Submit a structured request with the property, project or task location and the outcome you want.</p><h2>2. We define the scope</h2><p>We review the request, identify whether specialist professionals are required, and set out exactly what we will and will not do.</p><h2>3. Receive a written quotation</h2><p>Our professional fees and identifiable third-party costs are stated clearly in Ghana cedis before work begins.</p><h2>4. We assign the right person</h2><p>A trained representative or appropriately qualified professional carries out the agreed work.</p><h2>5. We verify and document</h2><p>Relevant evidence is collected, reviewed and linked to the job record.</p><h2>6. You receive the report</h2><p>You receive clear findings and supporting evidence. Where further action is required, important decisions remain yours.</p></div></section>
